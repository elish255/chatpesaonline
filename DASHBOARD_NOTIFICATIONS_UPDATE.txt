USER DASHBOARD NOTIFICATIONS UPDATE

- The newest notification is shown prominently as the main notification card.
- All older notifications are hidden from the main dashboard.
- A bell button appears below the header.
- The red badge shows the number of older notifications.
- Tapping the bell opens a scrollable notifications panel.
- Each older notification can be dismissed individually.
- The main/latest notification can also be dismissed with its X button.
- Existing withdrawal, chat, balance, and authentication functionality is unchanged.
